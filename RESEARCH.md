## Feature matrix → Prime.Health
- **Tools:** 26 calculators (vs. 4–28 on leaders) + Prime Score + screening planner.
- **Lead capture:** quiz result → plan (Noom/Levels/PN), free guide magnet (Harvard/Attia), newsletter
  everywhere (all), partner match consent (Zocdoc/Psychology Today model).
- **Trust:** editorial policy, sources on every page, disclaimer, review dates (Healthline/MNT/Sleep
  Foundation). No invented reviewer names, testimonials or user counts.
- **Monetization:** AdSense (WebMD/Drugs.com/Patient), sponsorship + advertise page (MNT/Huberman),
  donations with impact framing (AHA/ADA/Mayo), contests (ADA events), lead partners.
- **Design:** search-first utility (Drugs.com), pillars (Oura/Attia), lab-report range bars (unique),
  accessibility baseline (NHS/CDC).
