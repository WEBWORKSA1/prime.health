Our mission

Health advice is everywhere; clear, trustworthy numbers are not. We turn clinical guidelines and research into free tools and plain-language guides so anyone can see where they stand, what to change and when to talk to a professional.

What we publish

  • Calculators that show their formulas, ranges and limits.
  • The Prime Score, a 3-minute healthspan check.
  • Guides grounded in guidelines and systematic reviews.
  • Health Pulse and a video library refreshed daily from reputable sources.

Principles

  • Evidence first. Every page lists its sources. See our editorial policy.
  • Privacy by design. Calculations run in your browser; saved numbers stay on your device.
  • No diagnosis. We help you understand numbers; clinicians diagnose and treat.
  • Independence. Advertisers and sponsors never influence content or results.

How we're funded

Display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, a consent-based partner network that connects visitors who ask for help with vetted professionals, and reader support. We do not sell personal data.

Independent

Prime.Health is an independent website. It is not affiliated with any hospital, clinic, insurer or company using a similar name. See our trademark and copyright disclosure.
