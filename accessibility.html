Accessibility statement

Last updated November 11, 2026

We want Prime.Health to work for everyone. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we do

  • Keyboard access to every control, with visible focus and a skip-to-content link.
  • Labels on every form field and live announcements for calculator results.
  • Text contrast designed for at least 4.5:1, plus a dark theme.
  • Charts and range bars include text alternatives with the value and category.
  • Animations respect the reduced-motion setting.
  • Pages work when zoomed to 200% and on small screens.

Known limitations

Embedded third-party videos may not meet every criterion, and some older PDFs we link to are outside our control.

Feedback

If anything is hard to use, tell us through the contact form. We aim to respond within two business days.
