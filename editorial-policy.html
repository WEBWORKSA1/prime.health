Editorial policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Prime.Health publishes health calculators and guides that people use to make real decisions. These standards explain how we earn that trust.

How we choose sources

We rank evidence in this order and cite it on every page:

  1. Clinical practice guidelines from major bodies (for example WHO, CDC, NIH, U.S. Preventive Services Task Force, American Heart Association and American College of Cardiology, American Diabetes Association, NICE).
  2. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses in peer-reviewed journals.
  3. Large randomised controlled trials.
  4. Large prospective cohort studies, clearly described as observational.

We avoid press releases as primary sources, do not rely on single small studies for advice, and say plainly when evidence is limited or mixed.

Calculators

Every calculator states its formula, the guideline behind its ranges, and its limits. Results are estimates for education, not diagnoses. Calculations run in your browser.

Writing standards

  • Plain language, short sentences and defined terms.
  • Absolute numbers alongside relative risks when we can.
  • No promises of cures, no fear-based headlines and no promotion of unproven treatments.
  • Clear signposting to professional care, including when symptoms need urgent attention.

Review and updates

Each page shows the date it was last updated. We review every page at least once a year and promptly when a major guideline changes. Licensed clinicians are being recruited as medical reviewers (see Careers); when a page has been clinically reviewed, the reviewer's name, credentials and review date will appear on it.

Use of software and AI

We use software tools, including AI writing assistants, to help research, draft and check content. A human editor checks every page against its cited sources before publishing, and we do not publish personalised medical advice.

Independence from advertisers

Advertising and sponsorship are clearly labelled and kept separate from editorial content. Sponsors cannot change formulas, results, rankings or wording. We do not accept advertising that conflicts with our advertising standards.

Automated feeds

Health Pulse and the video library refresh automatically from public sources. We show headlines and titles with links to the original, and inclusion is not endorsement.

Corrections

Found an error? Tell us through the contact form (topic: correction). We review every report, fix verified errors quickly and note significant corrections on the page.
