Questions

Is Prime.Health free?

Yes. Every calculator, guide and the Prime Score are free with no sign-up. The site is funded by advertising, sponsorship and reader support.

Do you store what I type into the calculators?

No. Calculations run in your browser. If you press Save to My Numbers, results are stored only on your device. We only receive information you choose to send through a form.

Where do the formulas and ranges come from?

From clinical guidelines and peer-reviewed research, such as the 2025 AHA/ACC blood pressure guideline, the American Diabetes Association and the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force. Every tool lists its sources.

Can Prime.Health diagnose a condition?

No. The tools are educational and help you understand your numbers. Diagnosis and treatment need a qualified health professional.

Is Prime.Health affiliated with other companies called Prime Health?

No. Prime.Health is an independent website with no connection to any company, clinic or product using a similar name. See our trademark disclosure.
