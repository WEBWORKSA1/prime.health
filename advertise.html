Advertising standards

  • All paid content is labelled “Advertisement” or “Sponsored”.
  • Sponsors have no control over editorial content, formulas, rankings or results.
  • We do not accept ads for unapproved drugs or supplements making disease claims, “miracle” weight-loss products, tobacco or vaping, gambling or anything that conflicts with Google publisher policies.
  • Health claims in sponsored material must be truthful and supported by evidence.
