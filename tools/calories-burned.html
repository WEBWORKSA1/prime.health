How it works

A MET (metabolic equivalent) is a multiple of resting energy use. Sitting quietly is 1 MET; brisk walking is about 4.3. The Compendium of Physical Activities lists measured MET values for hundreds of activities.

kcal per minute = MET × 3.5 × weight (kg) ÷ 200

Reading your result

The headline is gross calories, which includes what you would burn at rest anyway. The “above resting” figure subtracts that and is the fairer number if you are matching exercise against food. Activities at 3–5.9 METs count as moderate and 6 or more as vigorous in physical activity guidelines.

Limits to keep in mind

MET values are averages for adults. Fitness level, technique, terrain and heat can change your real burn by 20% or more. Fitness trackers have similar error ranges.

Questions people ask

Should I eat back the calories I burn?

Partly, if you are training hard or losing weight fast. Many people overestimate exercise calories, so start with about half.

What burns the most calories?

Sustained vigorous activities like running, rowing and fast cycling. The best choice is the one you will do consistently.

Does strength training burn many calories?

Fewer during the session than cardio, but it builds muscle that supports metabolic health and healthy ageing.
