How it works

Sleep debt = Σ (nightly need − hours slept)

Extra sleep on some nights offsets shortfalls on others. Count actual sleep, not time in bed.

Reading your result

A few hours of debt is common and recoverable. Pay it back with 30–60 extra minutes a night by going to bed earlier, keeping your wake-up time fixed. Long weekend lie-ins shift your body clock and make Monday harder.

Limits to keep in mind

Chronic short sleep is not fully undone by catch-up sleep. If you need much more than 9 hours or feel sleepy despite enough time in bed, check for sleep disorders such as sleep apnoea.

Questions people ask

Can you really repay sleep debt?

Short-term debt, yes, partly. The better strategy is to stop accruing it.

How do I know my sleep need?

On a holiday without alarms, the amount you settle at after a few days is a good estimate. Most adults land between 7 and 9 hours.

Do sleep trackers measure sleep accurately?

They estimate total sleep reasonably well but are less reliable for sleep stages.
