The RDA of 0.8 g per kg prevents deficiency. Building or keeping muscle, especially after 50, usually takes more.

Reading your result

Split the total across three or four meals. About 0.4 g per kg per meal (roughly 25–40 g for most adults) maximises muscle building at each sitting. Breakfast is usually the gap: eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, tofu or a protein-rich smoothie close it.

Limits to keep in mind

People with chronic kidney disease may need less protein; check with a clinician. Needs during pregnancy and breastfeeding are higher and should be set individually.

Questions people ask

Is high protein bad for your kidneys?

In healthy kidneys, intakes up to about 2 g per kg have not been shown to cause harm. With existing kidney disease, follow medical advice.

Can I get enough protein on a plant-based diet?

Yes. Combine legumes, soy, grains, nuts and seeds, and aim for the upper end of your range.

Does protein timing matter?

Total daily intake matters most; spreading it evenly helps a little more.
