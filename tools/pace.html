How it works

Pace is time divided by distance. Predictions use Pete Riegel's 1981 endurance model, which says time grows slightly faster than distance:

T₂ = T₁ × (D₂ ÷ D₁)^1.06

Reading your result

Use the highlighted row to sanity-check your goal. If your training rarely goes beyond 10 km, add 5–10% to the half-marathon and marathon predictions. Easy runs should feel conversational, usually 60–90 seconds per km slower than 10K race pace.

Limits to keep in mind

Heat, hills, wind and fuelling all change real results. Predictions assume you have trained for the target distance.

Questions people ask

What is a good 5K time?

Anything you can improve on. Recreational runners commonly finish between 25 and 40 minutes.

How accurate is the Riegel formula?

Good from 5K to half marathon for trained runners; it tends to be optimistic for first marathons.

How do I convert pace per km to per mile?

Multiply by 1.609. The calculator shows both.
