How it works

Ovulation usually happens about 14 days before the next period, because the phase after ovulation is fairly constant. So:

Ovulation ≈ period start + cycle length − 14 days

The fertile window is the five days before ovulation plus the day of ovulation, because sperm can survive several days.

Reading your result

The two days before ovulation and the day itself carry the highest chance of conception. If cycles vary by more than a few days, use LH ovulation test strips from a few days before the predicted date.

Limits to keep in mind

Calendar methods are estimates and should not be used for contraception. If you are under 35 and have not conceived after 12 months of trying (6 months if 35 or older), talk with a clinician.

Questions people ask

Can I get pregnant outside the fertile window?

Unlikely but possible, because ovulation timing varies from cycle to cycle.

Do apps predict ovulation better?

Apps that use only dates have similar accuracy. Those that add temperature or LH test data do better.

What if my cycles are irregular?

Calendar estimates are less reliable. Ovulation tests and a clinician visit help.
